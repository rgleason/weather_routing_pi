# Qualification for 1.37.0 / 2.4.0

The final Linux builds passed 677 production checks and 765 development checks. One optional ocean timing test is disabled in each suite; actual-host ocean controls were run separately.

The 501-case corpus contains 142 real-GRIB scenarios and 16 negative controls. All six methods were screened in 3,006 fresh processes with deliberately short solver deadlines. There were 1,741 validated method results, covering 456 cases, with no process errors, negative-control acceptances or completed tracks failing fresh independent replay. Timeouts, missing coverage and infeasible inputs remain recorded as failures; this does not claim every passage completes within three or six seconds.

The final screen of the shipping typed automatic controller comprised 1,002 Quick/Quick2 method trials with nearby-only prefix capture. There were 842 independently replayed complete results and 26 recovery successes, including 11 real-GRIB trials. Successful recovery had a median of 16.4 ms, 95th percentile of 132.3 ms and maximum of 191.5 ms under concurrent load. These are method recoveries, not 26 additional Auto passages: Quick2 already succeeds in many cases where Quick needs help. A separate screen using the original numerical inputs produced 65 validated recoveries in 1,002 trials, including 12 real-GRIB trials. Neither screen accepted a negative control or failed independent replay.

Seventeen longer controls run with and without the separate handoff experiment retained identical acceptance and ETA outcomes. The experiment triggered once and the failed route remained honestly failed. It stays off by default. Unit tests cover invalid early prefixes, full-route replay, motor-time limits, no invented motoring, child and parent cancellation, Quick3 identity and attempt history, Auto early return, All continuing comparisons and rejection of faraway prefixes before copying.

The actual OpenCPN Hanalei-to-Nuku-Hiva control used a fixed NOAA forecast and departure, standard boat, Full GSHHG, no currents and 0.1 NM clearance. Production retained its Quick2 ETA of 2026-12-01T04:55:53Z. Development retained its corrected forecast-boundary ETA of 2026-12-03T13:06:49Z and explicitly reported 1 h 06 min of climatology wind after forecast expiry. Each branch's existing wind interpolation is preserved.

Full-passage prefix capture initially added excessive work. The final 40 NM admission restored failed Quick search to 59.9 seconds and Quick2 to 11.8 seconds on the production control; the earlier byte-cache-only control took 59.4 and 10.7 seconds. Both returned the same track and ETA. Timings vary with load. The byte-governed frame cache showed no eviction/reload churn and retains its physical/process RAM guards.

A Holyhead/Conwy actual-host case used explicitly synthetic 15-knot wind and a synthetic sail polar with real Full shoreline data. All retained a validated Quick3 candidate while keeping an equal-ETA Quick2 winner. The comparison UI selected Quick3, checked its display name and computed identity, and switched back without recalculation. Normal host close exited successfully. Testing also exposed and corrected a settings refresh after host config teardown and a test-only exit path that bypassed normal plugin deinitialisation. The 1.36-to-1.37 upgrade retains user boats, polars and route configurations without the legacy import prompt.

The development repository's `tools/adaptive-settings-lab` preserves the reproduction harness. Local evidence is in `output/routing-completion-pass-20261118` in the OpenCPN workspace: per-trial records, corpus/binary SHA256 receipts, source hashes, full test logs, host inputs and results, and `qualification-summary.json`. Vendored solver `.cpp` files are unchanged; the archive helper's optional admission parameter and authorised adapter/controller changes are explicitly qualified in the development freeze manifest.

These are Linux qualification results. Cross-platform CI, working-profile installation and public publication are separate steps.
